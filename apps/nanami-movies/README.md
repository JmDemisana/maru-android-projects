# Nanami Movies

Nanami Movies is an Android movie, series, and cartoon player with SIMKL synchronization.

[Download the Rotator APK](https://github.com/JmDemisana/maru-android-projects/releases/download/nanami-movies-rotator/nanami-movies-rotator.apk)

## Highlights in 0.2.0

* **Netflix OLED interface.** Browse featured billboards with title logos, Top 10 numbered rows, Continue Watching resume cards, category chips, and rich title details.
* **Shared Nanami streaming engine.** Switch video sources while loading, scrub smoothly on the 60fps canvas timeline with IntroDB chapter markers, adjust brightness and volume with vertical gestures, export clips in Clip Studio, and cast with Chromecast.
* **SIMKL account linking and Guest Mode.** Connect SIMKL accounts through an instant WebView PKCE flow or device PIN code, switch between saved accounts, queue watchlist or watch progress changes while offline, or track locally in Guest Mode.
* **Private offline vault.** Queue single movies or batch season downloads into app private storage with cellular protection and optional WiFi seeding.

## Installation and updates

Open the APK on your Android device to install it. Inside the app, go to **Account** and tap **Check for Updates** to verify and install newer builds, or **Generate Log Dump** to inspect service connectivity.

## Release channels

* **Rotator.** Development builds from `main` publish to the rolling `nanami-movies-rotator` prerelease with `SHA256SUMS.txt` and `update.json`.
* **Stable.** Official tagged builds (`nanami-movies-vX.Y.Z`) promote a tested Rotator run through **Nanami Movies Completer** and update `nanami-movies-latest`.
