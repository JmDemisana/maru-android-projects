# Nanami Movies

Nanami Movies is an Android movie, series, and cartoon player with SIMKL synchronization.

[Download the latest stable APK (v1.0.0)](https://github.com/JmDemisana/maru-android-projects/releases/download/nanami-movies-latest/nanami-movies-latest.apk)

## Highlights in 1.0.0

* **Netflix OLED interface.** Browse full bleed featured billboards with transparent title logos, Top 10 numbered rows, Continue Watching resume cards, category chips, unified Search, and clean title details with a 3 column More Like This grid.
* **Consumer first playback engine.** Clean dark loading screen with live buffering progress, translucent half screen in video Playback Settings with Video Sources (`Source 1` through `Source N`), 60fps canvas timeline with IntroDB chapter markers, vertical brightness and volume gestures, Clip Studio export, and Chromecast support.
* **SIMKL account linking and Guest Mode.** Connect one or more SIMKL accounts via browser Custom Tab PKCE sign in or 8 character PIN code, switch accounts anytime, sync Watching, Plan to Watch, Completed, On Hold, Dropped, and ratings automatically, or track everything locally in Guest Mode.
* **Private offline vault.** Download individual movies or batch unwatched seasons into app private storage with cellular protection and optional WiFi seeding.

## Installation and updates

Open the APK on your Android device to install it. Inside the app, go to **Account** and tap **Check for Updates** to verify and install newer stable builds, or **Generate Log Dump** to inspect service connectivity.

## Release channels

* **Stable.** Official tagged builds (`nanami-movies-vX.Y.Z`) publish through **Nanami Movies Completer** and update `nanami-movies-latest`.
* **Rotator.** Development builds from `main` publish to the rolling `nanami-movies-rotator` prerelease with `SHA256SUMS.txt` and `update.json`.
